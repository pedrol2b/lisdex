# 0011 — Menu bar: standard-orientation fragment by default; app icon stays Figma A

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-11-19). Size amended by 0012 (18 pt).
- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Supersedes:** 0009, 0010. **Amends:** 0003.

## Context

Three menu bar attempts on 2026-11-19:
- Figma 22 × 22: too small (~16 × 11 visible).
- Rotated fragment (0009): good size, but doesn't look like the molecule's standard drawing.
- Full horizontal skeleton (0010): faithful, but too long (~52 pt).

The full formula is ~3.3:1. At 120° bond angles it can't be both complete and ~2:1. A folded full layout (lysine chain rising from Cα) fits ~1.3:1, but in the menu bar it's tiny and as an app icon it's lopsided. Comparison sheet shown to the owner.

## Decision

- **Menu bar default (B / `minimal`):** the left part of the standard horizontal depiction, in the same orientation as the PubChem/Wikipedia drawing: phenyl → CH₂ → CH(CH₃ down) → NH → C(=O down) → Cα(NH₂ up). 14 of 19 heavy atoms; the (CH₂)₄NH₂ lysine tail is omitted. **≈ 30.7 × 16 pt.**
- **Menu bar option (A / `faithful`):** the complete horizontal skeleton, 19 heavy atoms, ≈ 51.5 × 16 pt (Settings, Phase 4).
- Both are generated by `scripts/lisdexamfetamine-skeleton.py`, which asserts the atom count and 120° at every atom. Strokes 1.25 / 1.6 pt; ring outlined / filled; shared frame per variant.
- The popover's Keep Awake tile uses B.
- **App icon: unchanged.** Figma A artwork (full molecule, folded to fit the square, gold on graphite) from ADR 0003.
- The rotated drawing and the Figma 22 pt menu bar grid are retired. The Figma file stays the record of the original iteration.

## Consequences

- Amphetamine-sized status item that reads as "the start of the lisdexamfetamine drawing". The full molecule remains one setting away and in the app icon.
- Menu bar and app icon differ in layout but share palette, stroke style and the phenyl-left composition.

## Alternatives considered

- Folded full molecule in the menu bar — ~20 × 16 pt, ring too small.
- Full horizontal at 14 pt — still ~45 pt long.
- Matching the app icon to the menu bar fragment — loses the complete molecule from the identity.
